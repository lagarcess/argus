# Synthetic ingestion fixture kit and local harness

## 1. Why
Serve MVEE sections 4, 5 and 12: capture, review, explicit confirmation,
saved records and corrections. Synthetic documents establish mechanics only;
they provide no evidence of real Dominican bank extraction accuracy.

## 2. Locked decisions
1. Own only tests/synthetic_ingestion/ and scoped documentation/evidence.
2. Keep factories, renderers, provisional proposals and local persistence outside production imports.
3. Seed Faker for incidental details; deliberate Decimal scenarios own money relationships.
4. Preserve DOP/USD, source locations, unresolved fields and destination ambiguity.
5. Actual CSV/PDF-byte parsing is reported separately from explicit authored extraction stubs.
6. Confirm reliable rows explicitly; retain exceptions, exact-source retry identity,
   potential overlap review and distinct legitimate similar purchases.
7. Corrections retain history and change only harness-derived totals.
8. No household permissions are modeled. Destination is a resolved test label only.

## 3. Reserved scope
No production routes, schema, RLS, auth, prompts, calculations, analytics,
disposable demo, customer data, paid services, OCR/STT provider selection,
bank scraping, wallets or production storage. Audio/STT remains untested unless
local speech synthesis works; synthesis would not validate transcription.

## 4. Contract gates
No canonical API/data contract changes. All formats are provisional scaffolding.
Document commands, capabilities, limitations and future consented-document checklist
in tests/synthetic_ingestion/README.md; evidence under
 docs/reports/evidence/synthetic-ingestion/.
