# Research notes

These are the dated research notes behind the Dominican bank-connectivity report. Research agents wrote them on 2026-09-27 from public sources only. Each note states its method, labels every claim, and cites a URL with the date it was seen. The report files one level up are the synthesis. When a report file states that the report author re-read a source, that source was checked independently of these notes.

The notes are a record of what was found that day. They are not maintained, and they are not legal advice.
