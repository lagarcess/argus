### Reading of the one line 48 failure on `2520da49` (input for the diagnosis worker, not proven)

Arithmetic first. "Mar" joins at line 27, so the dinner is DOP 2,000 over five people, 400 each. Tú 500 + Ana 300 + 3 × 400 = 2,000. Save is disabled when `!valid`, any share is −1, or the shares do not sum to `cents` (editor line 109). So the failure means one of: a share field held a wrong number, a share field carried an error string (`hasAmountError`), or a member was dropped.

Ranked:

1. **Likeliest: a typed share was corrupted by `CanvasMoneyValueInput`'s re-sync under load** (`ios/ArgusFoundation/Cuadrao/CanvasMoneyValueInput.swift:19-23`, not in this delta). Line 48 has failed before for the money-keystroke defect (W1b; fixed by `00b5be65` for `CanvasDecimalInput`). That fix defers binding writes and guards `updateUIView` with `pending == 0`. The wrapper still has a hole by reading: `value` trails `raw` by one update pass (`onChange(of: raw) { value = numericValue }`), and `onChange(of: value) { if numericValue != value { syncValue() } }` reads the live `raw`. If the next keystroke's deferred write lands between those two passes, `raw` is "50" while `value` is 5, the mismatch is treated as an external change, `syncValue()` writes `raw = "5.00"`, and with `pending == 0` the coordinator accepts it and rewrites the field. The next digit then gives a wrong amount or a third decimal ("Revisa los decimales para DOP"), either of which disables Save. This needs keystrokes bunching against slow update passes, which is what a contended full suite gives and a solo run does not. It is independent of the overlay, so the revert would not cure it.
2. **`replace` helper fragility** (test line 70): one coordinate tap at dx 0.98, a delete count taken from `field.value` at that instant, then the text. No wait for focus, no read-back of the field. A dropped delete or a caret not at the end leaves for example "4300". Same symptom as 1, and 1 may be its mechanism.
3. **Overlay took a touch during `reveal`** (the author's suggestion). The swipe starts at the screen centre, which was inside the overlay on an exact-split row. A swipe has to register as a tap for this; a pan normally cancels `onTapGesture`. Possible, least likely. Note it could also have happened at the reveals on lines 35 or 41 (a member dropped there gives 1,800 at line 48); dropping Ana would fail at line 45 instead.
4. Save enablement lag is unlikely: each keystroke commits within one main-queue hop, and `wait(for:toEqual:timeout: 2)` evaluates at least once after the last swipe.
5. Keyboard covering Ana's field would fail at line 45 with a focus error, not at line 48.

Cheapest discriminator: on failure at line 48, record the `value` of the five `Parte de …` fields, the remainder caption ("Todo cuadra." / "Por repartir" / "de más"), any `-error` text, and the five member button labels. A wrong field value or error points to 1 or 2; an "excluido" label points to 3; all correct with Save disabled points to 4. A focused repro of 1 is burst `typeText` into a share field while the main thread is loaded, then a read-back of the field.
