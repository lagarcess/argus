Assuming DOP 1,000,000 represents face value purchased at par, Banco Popular’s stated 10.5% coupon would generate DOP 105,000 in contractual coupon income per year. The equivalent monthly average is DOP 8,750, although the bond’s actual payments are semiannual rather than monthly.

### What this calculation assumes

This illustration uses Banco Popular Dominicano’s subordinated-bond issue maturing on 2026-11-28. Its published terms state a 10.50% coupon and semiannual payments.

| Input | Value |
|---|---:|
| Face value | DOP 1,000,000 |
| Coupon rate used as the income rate | 10.5% |
| Annual coupon income | DOP 105,000 |
| Monthly equivalent | DOP 8,750 |

**Formula:** annual coupon income = face value × coupon rate.

### Important limitation

This is coupon income at par, not a verified current-market return. A public page stating the bond’s executable price or yield to maturity as of 2026-09-13 could not be found. If bought above or below face value, the amount invested would purchase a different face value, and the return through maturity would differ from the coupon rate.

The published terms also describe the issue as subordinated, without collateral and outside the deposit guarantee. Because its stated maturity is 2026-11-28, the actual cash received by a new buyer would additionally depend on the settlement price, accrued interest and whether the security remained available for purchase; those current inputs could not be verified.
