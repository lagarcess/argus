# guest-q1-en

How much should I save each month if I earn 38,000 pesos and want an iPad?

Assuming you mean Mexican pesos, start with no savings, earn no interest, and want to pay cash after 12 monthly deposits, you would need to save MXN 980.75 per month for the standard iPad or MXN 1,774.92 per month for the iPad Air.

### Price examples

| Example | Listed cash price | Monthly saving period | Required monthly saving |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Standard iPad | MXN 11,769 | 12 months | MXN 980.75 |
| iPad Air | MXN 21,299 | 12 months | MXN 1,774.92 |

The standard iPad listing was updated on 2026-09-09. The iPad Air listing was updated on 2026-08-31. The required amount changes if “pesos” means another country’s currency, you want a different model, or you choose a different deadline.

### Assumptions behind the calculation

- Currency: Mexican pesos.
- Current savings toward the purchase: MXN 0.
- Interest earned while saving: 0%.
- Deposits are made at the end of each month.
- Accessories, delivery charges and future price changes are excluded.
- Your stated income is MXN 38,000, but the monthly saving calculation uses the product price and deadline; it does not assess affordability because your expenses and existing savings were not provided.
