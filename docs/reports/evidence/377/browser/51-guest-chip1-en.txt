How does Netflix make money?

Netflix makes money mainly by charging recurring monthly subscription fees. Advertising on its lower-priced ad-supported plan is a smaller but growing second revenue stream.

REVENUE SOURCE	HOW IT WORKS
Subscriptions	Members pay monthly for plans that vary by advertising, video quality, simultaneous streams and other features. Revenue grows when Netflix adds members, reduces cancellations or raises prices.
Advertising	Advertisers pay to show commercials to members using the ad-supported plan. Netflix earns both the member’s lower subscription fee and advertising revenue.
Extra-member access	In supported markets, account holders can pay an additional fee for someone outside their household. This converts some password sharing into revenue.
Minor/indirect sources	Consumer products, licensing and other partnerships contribute, but they are not the core business. Games and most live programming primarily make the service more attractive rather than generating separate user fees.
How the business turns revenue into profit

Netflix spends heavily to produce, license and market films, series, live events and games. Because the same content can be delivered digitally to millions of additional viewers at relatively low incremental cost, higher subscription and advertising revenue can improve profit once content and operating costs are covered.

Content serves several purposes:

attracting new members;
retaining existing members;
supporting price increases by improving perceived value;
creating more viewing inventory for advertisements;
generating attention and sign-ups through live events.

In 2025, Netflix generated $45.183 billion in revenue, $13.327 billion in operating profit and $10.981 billion in net income. For Q2 2026, reported on July 16, 2026, revenue was $12.560 billion. Netflix said growth was driven primarily by membership and pricing growth, plus higher advertising revenue.

Netflix does not publicly disclose a precise current dollar split between subscription and advertising revenue, so an exact live percentage for each is unavailable. As of August 9, 2026, subscriptions remain the economic core, while advertising is an increasingly important additional source.
