# Research grounding spot-check

The English and Spanish browser turns asked how Netflix makes money and explicitly requested sources. Both rendered substantive answers with a five-source panel. Screenshots and source snapshots are in `browser/`.

The primary-source check used the [Netflix annual reports index](https://ir.netflix.net/financials/annual-reports-and-proxies/default.aspx) and its linked [2025 annual report](https://s22.q4cdn.com/959853165/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/99482238-46b2-4d0d-b292-40e6781bdf03.pdf).

- PDF page 4, printed page 1, supports monthly membership fees as the main revenue source.
- PDF page 23, printed page 20, reports 2025 revenue of $45.183 billion and says revenue outside monthly membership fees was not material. This supports the answers' subscription-led characterization and revenue figure.
- PDF page 54, printed page 51, reports 2025 content amortization of $16.422 billion, supporting the Spanish answer's rounded figure.

This is a spot-check of central claims against the primary report, not an exhaustive audit of every linked article or every sentence. External source titles retain their published language. No research figure was used to seed a backtest.
