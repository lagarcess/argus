# Founder DOCN side-by-side

These exact steps are prepared for an approved PR preview. No hosted preview
was provisioned, and the local proof servers are stopped. The completed
targeted comparison failed the writing/truth bar; a successful founder
side-by-side remains unproven. A provider-free replay proves rendering only
and cannot substitute for this comparison.

## English

1. Open the candidate preview and sign in to the approved test account.
2. Open Settings, then Preferences, then App language; choose English.
3. Start a new conversation and send exactly:
   `buy and hold DOCN since September 2023 against SPY`
4. If Argus asks for capital or dates, use `$1,000 starting capital` and
   `September 1, 2023 through September 9, 2026`. Confirm the displayed
   effective window, DOCN, SPY, and buy-and-hold setup before Run backtest.
   The explicit end date makes the two language examples comparable.
5. Read the Quick take inside its existing frame, then click Explain result
   to open Breakdown. Keep the result card and both readout frames visible
   for the competitor comparison.
6. Reload: both saved texts should remain identical. Switch the workspace to
   Spanish: both frames should use Spanish templates. Switching back should
   restore the same saved English model text without another model request.

## Español

1. En la vista previa del candidato, abre Ajustes, Preferencias e Idioma de
   la app; selecciona Español.
2. Inicia una conversación nueva y envía exactamente:
   `Compra y mantén DOCN desde septiembre de 2023 y compáralo con SPY.`
3. Si Argus pide capital o fechas, responde:
   `Capital inicial de $1,000, del 1 de septiembre de 2023 al 9 de septiembre de 2026.`
   Comprueba DOCN, SPY, compra y mantenimiento, capital y período efectivo;
   selecciona Ejecutar backtest.
4. Lee Lectura rápida y pulsa Explicar resultado para abrir Desglose.
5. Recarga y comprueba que el texto guardado no cambió. Cambia a inglés:
   aparecerán las plantillas en inglés. Al volver a español debe reaparecer
   el mismo texto del modelo, sin otra llamada al modelo.

## What to compare

Use the same symbols, dates, starting capital and cost assumptions in the
competitor. Judge meaning beyond the card: benchmark tradeoff, roughness,
what a drawdown of that size illustrates in money, and useful deeper context
from the stored annualized return, volatility, Sharpe, costs or activity.
The two frames should complement one another. A starting-capital drawdown
illustration must not claim to be the actual historical peak-to-trough dollar
loss. Neither frame should give advice, a forecast, or an unsourced explanation
of why the price moved.

A screenshot of a template fallback demonstrates safe behavior, but it does
not satisfy the model-writing comparison. Record the source/fallback fields,
candidate SHA and actual effective window beside the final evidence. Also
open the pre-lane saved result in both languages and confirm it remains on
the current templates.
