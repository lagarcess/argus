Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

This policy explains what Argus does with your account data, conversations, feedback, and the requests it sends to outside providers while the product is in early access.

Data we collect

Argus may collect account details, authentication state, conversations, investing ideas you submit, saved product records, feedback, support requests, browser and device basics, usage events, logs, and error diagnostics.

How we use data

We use data to run the product, authenticate users, save and restore conversations, generate and review historical simulations, debug failures, protect the service, respond to support, and improve Argus while it is in early access.

AI and market data providers

Conversation content and prompts may be sent to OpenRouter and model providers so Argus can interpret requests and draft responses. Market data and asset discovery may use providers such as Alpaca and Kraken. Provider availability can affect supported symbols, time windows, and results.

Infrastructure and product services

Argus uses Supabase for authentication and product persistence, Render for hosting, OpenRouter for model access, and Cloudflare for domain delivery and bot protection. Product analytics through PostHog run on Argus servers, and only when they are intentionally enabled. TradingView Lightweight Charts is used as a charting library with attribution; it is not the source of Argus market data.

Cookies and browser storage

Argus stores a small amount of data in your browser. All of it either keeps the product working or remembers your settings, and none of it is advertising or tracking, which is why Argus does not show a cookie consent banner.

Sign-in cookies
These keep you signed in as you move between pages. Supabase authentication sets them, and Argus refreshes them while you keep using the product. They stay after you close the browser, and signing out clears them. Signing in or resetting your password also sets a short-lived cookie that completes the exchange securely, discarded once it finishes. If you block them, signing in will not work.
Guest handoff cookies
If you start as a guest and then create an account, Argus sets two short-lived cookies so your temporary conversation can move onto the new account. They last ten minutes, are limited to Argus authentication routes, and are cleared as soon as the handoff finishes.
Security cookies
Cloudflare Turnstile checks that sign-in and guest requests come from a person rather than a bot, and Cloudflare may set short-lived challenge cookies to do that. They are scoped to Argus and are not used to follow you across other sites.
Preferences you set
Your browser’s local storage holds your language, which starts from your browser’s own setting until you change it, along with your appearance and layout choices, tips you dismissed, and the conversations you marked temporary. This stays on your device and Argus never sends it to advertisers.

Argus does not use advertising cookies, ad pixels, marketing trackers, or cross-site tracking, and it does not share browser data with advertising partners. Product analytics, when enabled, run on Argus servers and store nothing in your browser. You can clear or block this storage in your browser settings, though signing in will stop working if you block the sign-in cookies.

Access and retention

Founder and administrator access is limited to operating, debugging, securing, and improving Argus during early access. We keep data while your account is active, while needed for support or security, or while required by operational records.

Your controls

You can archive or delete conversations in the app. While Argus is in early access, account deletion is handled by a person through the in-app request or by contacting support@get-argus.com.

Sale and sharing of personal information

Argus does not sell or share your personal information, and it does not use it for targeted advertising. If that ever changes, this policy and the controls in the product will be updated before it takes effect.
